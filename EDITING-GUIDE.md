# Editing your website

_A short guide for Becky. You don't need to know anything technical to use this._

## Getting in

1. Go to **your-website-address/admin** (bookmark this page).
2. Click **Login with GitHub** and sign in.

That's it. You're now looking at the editing panel.

## How it's organised

Down the left you'll see three groups:

| Group | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Website pages** | The wording on each page — Home, Meet Becky, How It Works, and so on. |
| **Practice details** | Your name, email, phone, hours, credentials, and the menu wording. Change something here and it updates everywhere on the site at once. |
| **Legal pages** | Consent, disclaimer, privacy, terms. Please check with your attorney before changing these. |

Click any page to open it. Each box is labelled in plain English — "Main headline", "Opening paragraph", and so on.

## Seeing your changes before they go live

As you type, a **preview appears beside the editor** showing roughly how it will look.

When you save, your change becomes a **draft** — it is _not_ on the live website yet. In the **Workflow** tab at the top you'll see your drafts, and each one gets a **preview link** showing the real website with your change in it. Have a look there before publishing.

## Publishing

When you're happy:

1. Open the draft in the **Workflow** tab.
2. Click **Publish**.

Your change goes to the live website in about **one to two minutes**. If you don't see it right away, wait a moment and refresh the page.

## Things that are good to know

- **Lists can be reordered.** Anywhere you see a list — questions, cards, opening hours — you can drag items to change their order, add new ones, or delete them.
- **Nothing is ever lost.** Every change is saved with a history, so anything can be undone. If something looks wrong, say so and it can be put back exactly as it was.
- **Don't worry about breaking it.** You can only change wording, not the design or the way the site works.

## Please take care with these

Some wording exists for safety or legal reasons:

- The **crisis note** (the 988 line) appears on every page. Please keep the number and the point that this website isn't a crisis service.
- The **privacy note on the contact form** asks people not to send health details. This keeps the website out of medical-privacy rules — please keep that meaning.
- The **legal pages** and the **small print in the footer** were written to protect you. Check with your attorney before changing them.

## If something goes wrong

Nothing you do here can permanently break the website. If a change doesn't look right, or you can't get logged in, just get in touch and it can be sorted out.
